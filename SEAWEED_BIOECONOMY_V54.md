# Planetary Restoration Model V54 — seaweed integration results

## What was restored from the earlier model

V54 explicitly restores the **grid/drone seaweed harvester** that existed in the earlier v18–v20 model and had been lost from V53.1. The central engineering screen uses a 100 wet-t/h unit, 10.70 kWh per dry tonne, and a **3.0x bankable efficiency factor** relative to the conventional-harvest proxy. The older 3.782x value is retained only as an engineering-upper movement proxy, not as a validated joule ratio.

## Central seaweed bioeconomy results

- Farm area: **0.586 million km²**.
- Dry biomass: **1.465 Gt/yr**.
- Grid-harvester electricity: **15.7 TWh/yr**, versus **47.0 TWh/yr** on the 3x conventional proxy; saving **31.3 TWh/yr**.
- Grid-harvester cost saving: **US$40.2 bn/yr** versus the earlier conventional harvest-cost reference.
- Finished low-carbon liquid fuel: **199.2 Mt/yr**, **2380 TWh/yr**.
- Aviation allocation: **1666 TWh/yr**, about **44.4%** of the earlier global aviation-energy benchmark.
- Heavy-equipment allocation: **595 TWh/yr**.
- Marine/other liquid fuel: **119 TWh/yr**.
- Potential lifecycle avoided fossil CO2 if fully additional: **0.440 GtCO2/yr** at the central 70% lifecycle reduction assumption.
- Food-grade seaweed: **73.3 Mt dry/yr**, equivalent to **170 million full-diet calorie-equivalents**, but the model treats it as a supplement across billions because species/iodine/trace-element constraints matter.
- Average standing-farm working carbon stock: **0.604 GtCO2-eq**.
- Two-year strategic fuel-reserve target: **1.256 GtCO2-eq**.
- Combined standing-farm + reserve working stock: **1.861 GtCO2-eq**. These are **not counted as permanent CDR**.
- Durable seaweed storage available inside the existing BiCRS allocation: **0.145 GtCO2/yr**, with about **2.93 Mha** of central dedicated-land relief.
- Physical farm habitat footprint: **586,000 km²**. The illustrative co-culture screen is **29.3 Mt/yr**, but it is deliberately **not credited** to the core fish-recovery result because habitat outcomes are site-specific.
- HTL/CSP process screen: **558 TWh/yr** electric/H2 plus **361 TWh-th/yr** direct concentrated-solar heat.

## Does it improve the planetary-restoration date?

The primary V54 accounting says **not automatically**. V53.1's emissions transition was technology-neutral and already assumed aviation/shipping and other energy sectors successfully decarbonize. Crediting seaweed fuel again would double-count the same mitigation. Therefore the primary deterministic accelerated-design milestone remains **2155**.

As a transparent additionality sensitivity:

- 50% of the modeled fuel displacement additional to V53.1: **2153**.
- 100% additional: **2151**.

That is an improvement of up to about **4 years** in the deterministic screen, while reducing required cumulative CDR. It is a sensitivity, not the new primary forecast.

## Scientific boundary

The grid harvester is an engineering screen, not a prototype-validated energy ratio. Macroalgae HTL yields, lifecycle GHG intensity, nutrient supply, farm ecology and very large-scale logistics all remain validation requirements. Seaweed-farm habitat effects are mixed across published studies, so V54 reports the habitat footprint but does not automatically translate all farm area into extra fish biomass.
