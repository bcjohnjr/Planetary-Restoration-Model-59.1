# V56 Model Scope and Limitations

## Scientific hierarchy

1. **FaIR 2.2.4 calibrated-constrained ensemble:** primary archived external carbon-cycle validation.
2. **Hector 3.5.0:** independent comparator, not OSCAR.
3. **V53.1 Joos four-reservoir carbon response:** diagnostic/controller model only under sustained large negative emissions.
4. Physical, economic, food, energy and governance modules: integrated screening layers with varying evidence maturity.

## Carbon-cycle limitation

No archived external model reaches 280 ppm within its tested horizon. V56 therefore has no externally validated restoration year. The Joos diagnostic reaches negative-reservoir and natural-outgassing regimes that make a symmetric linear impulse-response representation unsuitable as the sole headline carbon-cycle model for century-scale forced drawdown.

The 32.55 ppm FaIR-vs-internal difference near 2155 is not reducible to a simple extra-CDR number. Multiplying by 7.77 GtCO2/ppm gives an atmospheric concentration-equivalent diagnostic (~253 GtCO2), but ocean and land sinks respond dynamically and can reverse, so this is not an estimate of required additional removal.

## External benchmark extension

The source net-CO2 trajectory ends in 2183 after its maintenance window. Archived external runs extend the comparison by holding the 2183 annual net CO2 flux constant. This assumption is documented and must always be disclosed when showing post-2183 external results.

## Energy

The V53.1 pre-CDR transformed electricity base (86,181.327 TWh/yr) remains externally supplied and undecomposed by sector. Recovered tidal, wastewater, seaweed-processing and historical maritime electricity terms remain visible but are not automatically added or subtracted until the base is decomposed, preventing double counting.

All four V53.1 default synthetic grid-adequacy cases fail the strict reliability criterion. These are synthetic global screens, not a regional weather/transmission capacity-expansion model.

## Monetary system

Three of six V53.1 liquidity/redemption stress cases fail. The monetary architecture is a stress-screened design proposal, not proof of price stability, convertibility, sovereign adoption, purchasing-power stability or legal feasibility.

## Seaweed production

V56 primary seaweed production is nutrient-closed against explicitly credited eutrophic N/P only. The much larger 1.465 Gt dry/yr geometric potential requires additional natural or managed nutrient flux and remains a high engineering scenario.

The 2026 seaweed ecosystem-services meta-analysis biomass-accumulation median is reported as an ecological benchmark, not treated as a direct farm-harvest-yield ceiling.

## Seaweed fuel

Macroalgae ash is explicit (25% central; 20–35% sensitivity). Finished liquid-fuel yield is applied to ash-free dry organic feedstock. The HTL/upgrading yield, process-energy requirements and lifecycle displacement remain engineering screens requiring species/feedstock/process validation.

## Seaweed food

No universal metabolizable kcal/kg value is asserted. V56 reports protein and a protein-only energy floor plus an older gross-energy upper screen. Species, iodine, arsenic, heavy metals, sodium, digestibility, processing, palatability and safe inclusion rates require region- and species-specific food science.

## Fisheries

V56 fisheries is literature-bounded. It is not a global stock-by-stock population-dynamics model. A ~10-year recovery number is a median management sensitivity, not an ecosystem-wide deadline. Long-lived species, reefs and climate-stressed ecosystems can recover much more slowly.

## Population and welfare

Population numbers are scenario context, not an empirical carrying-capacity constant. Food, water, regional climate, biodiversity, materials, distribution, health systems, conflict and governance can become binding. V56 intentionally rounds population screening outputs instead of reporting false precision.

## Sea level, ice sheets and regional climate

The retained climate emulator is global mean and does not dynamically resolve regional climate, sea-level rise, ice sheets, permafrost geography or ocean circulation. Returning CO2 toward preindustrial values would not instantaneously restore ocean heat content or sea level.

## Publication gate

V56 remains NOT SUBMISSION READY until the carbon-cycle divergence is resolved/reframed and the OSCAR requirement is either completed or explicitly revised with scientific justification.
